Everything you need to know about our recycling process, products, and how we are making a difference in the Maldives.

Rashu Plastic Project is a Maldives-based initiative that transforms discarded plastic into beautiful, useful products. We collect, clean, shred, and remould plastic into items like keychains, earrings, and custom pieces, giving it a second life instead of letting it pollute our islands and oceans.

Our plastic comes from locally collected waste in Vaavu. We work to recover plastic that would otherwise end up in landfills or the ocean and turn it into durable, long-lasting products.

At the moment we work with HDPE, commonly found in bottle caps, containers, and household cosmetic items. Each type is carefully sorted and processed to ensure quality and safety.

Since we use real recycled plastic, no two batches are ever the same. The marbling, speckles, and colour blends are naturally created during the melting process, making every piece one-of-a-kind.

All plastic is saved and remelted into a new product. Nothing goes to waste in our workshop!
