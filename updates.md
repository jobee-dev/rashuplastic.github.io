## FAQs

What is Rashu Plastic Project?

- Rashu Plastic Project is a Maldives-based initiative that transforms discarded plastic into
    beautiful, useful products. We collect, clean, shred, and remould plastic into items like
    keychains, earrings, and custom pieces, giving it a second life instead of letting it pollute
    our islands and oceans.
- Where does your plastic come from?
- Our plastic comes from locally collected waste in Vaavu. We work to recover plastic
that would otherwise end up in landfills or the ocean and turn it into durable, long-lasting
products.
- What types of plastic do you use?
- At the moment we work with HDPE, commonly found in bottle caps, containers, and
household cosmetic items. Each type is carefully sorted and processed to ensure quality
and safety.
- Why do the colours and patterns vary?
- Since we use real recycled plastic, no two batches are ever the same. The marbling,
speckles, and colour blends are naturally created during the melting process, making
every piece one-of-a-kind.
- What happens to the leftover plastic in your recycling process?
- All plastic is saved and remelted into a new product
- Do you offer custom designs for resorts or businesses?
- Yes. We love collaborating with resorts, NGOs, and local businesses. We can create
custom shapes, branded pieces, and meaningful souvenirs, in your brand colours. made
from recycled plastic. Contact us to discuss your ideas.
- How does purchasing from Rashu Plastic Project make an impact?
- Every purchase helps:
- Reduce plastic waste in the Maldives
- Support local recycling innovation
- Promote circular design
- Support community and NGO collaborations
A portion of selected collections is also donated to local NGOs.
- How can I support Rashu Plastic Project?
You can support us by:


- Purchasing our products
- Sharing our work on social media
- Collaborating with us
- Partnering with us for workshops or events

##### Can I donate plastic to you?

- Not yet! At the moment we are currently only accepting plastic from Vaavu Atoll as we do
    not have large enough facilities to recycle larger quantities of plastic, we will be able to
    expand our recycling capacity in the future with better equipment.
- How can i purchase an item or organise a workshop?
- Get in touch with us directly (contact us page link)
For workshops we can travel across Maldives.
- Do you ship internationally?
- We currently deliver across all islands in the Maldives. For international shipping
inquires please contact us directly via email rashuplasticproject@gmail.com so we can do our
best to arrange it.

Workshops at Rashu Plastic Project - turn plastic waste into something to remember
At Rashu Plastic Project, our workshops are hands-on, creative, and impact-driven.
We invite guests, students, and community members to join us and experience the full journey
of recycling, from waste to wearable art.
This is not just a craft session.
It’s an experience in circular economy, island sustainability, and conscious creativity.
We can provide all resources and bring the session to you, all you need to provide is a well
ventilated space, tables and an electrical supply
- What makes our workshops different?
- We use real beach and community-collected plastic
- Participants learn about waste challenges in the Maldives


- Every piece made is unique
- You leave with something you created yourself
- You directly support a local sustainability project
Our workshops combine education, creativity, and meaningful impact.
Our Workshop Experiences

#### 1. Plastic to Product Experience (Signature Workshop)

A full introduction to small-scale plastic recycling.
You will:

- Learn about plastic waste in the Maldives
- Sort and understand different plastic types
- See how we shred and process plastic
- Press and mould your own keychain or jewellery piece
- Take home your handmade recycled product
Duration: 1.5 – 2 hours
Perfect for: Resort guests, eco-travellers, small groups

#### 2. Mini Creative Session

A shorter, lighter version of our main workshop.
You will:

- Learn the basics of plastic recycling
- Choose colours from pre-prepared flakes
- Press and finish your own keychain
Duration: 45–60 minutes


Perfect for: Guesthouses, cruise stops, quick island visits

## 3. School and Educational Programs

Designed for students of all ages.

Sessions can be adapted by age group and curriculum needs.
Duration and Location: Flexible to needs

# What you take home -

Every participant leaves with:
♻ A handmade recycled plastic product


🌱 A deeper understanding of plastic waste
🌊 A meaningful connection to local island sustainability
And most importantly, the knowledge that waste can have value.
Who can join?

- Tourists visiting Vaavu Atoll
- Resort guests (via collaboration)
- Schools and youth groups
- NGOs and sustainability programs
- Corporate or retreat groups
No previous experience needed.
Why it matters -
The Maldives faces serious challenges with plastic waste management. Many islands lack
advanced recycling systems, and much plastic is burned or transported long distances.
Our workshops demonstrate that:
Small-scale solutions are possible.
Local action matters.
Waste can be transformed.
When you join a Rashu workshop, you’re not just making something, you’re contributing to a
growing circular movement in island communities.
