Not just a craft session

It’s an experience in circular economy, island sustainability, and conscious creativity. We invite guests, students, and community members to join us and experience the full journey of recycling, from waste to wearable art.

What makes our workshops different?

  • We use real beach and community-collected plastic
  • Participants learn about waste challenges in the Maldives
  • Every piece made is unique
  • You directly support a local sustainability project

Why it matters

The Maldives faces serious challenges with plastic waste management. Many islands lack advanced recycling systems, and much plastic is burned or transported long distances.

Our workshops demonstrate that small-scale solutions are possible. Local action matters. Waste can be transformed.

When you join a Rashu workshop, you're not just making something, you're contributing to a growing circular movement in island communities.
